Local Storage Usage Policy

This app uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) to save information 
directly on your device. This data is not sent to our servers and is not shared 
with third parties.

We use local storage to:

- remember your display preferences (splash screen)
- save your progress within the app

This data:

- Stays entirely on your device
- Persists until you clear your browser's site data, or until we remove it
  programmatically.

You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data or 
cache for this app.

We do not use cookies or third-party tracking services.
